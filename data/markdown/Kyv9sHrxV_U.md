## aurelio-finance

[0:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Kyv9sHrxV_U&t=10)

- https://github.com/LosaLosSantos/aurelio-finance

Aurelio is a self-hosted wealth tracker that keeps all your financial records on your own computer. Track cash across banks and brokers, positions priced from the market with gains and dividends, and even what's inside your funds broken down by c country, sector, and currency. It handles homes, loans, cash flow, and savings goals. The optional AI chat answers from your own records, and anything it wants to change shows up as a card you confirm or reject.

## openpost

[0:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Kyv9sHrxV_U&t=39)

- https://github.com/getopenpost/openpost

Open Post puts the social media workflow in one place. Write a post, tailor it for each account, design a carousel, or edit a video, then schedule everything on a calendar. It also tracks results and can turn RSS updates or GitHub releases into drafts for review. You can self-host it with Docker, though publishing features depend on what each social platform allows.

## Haven

[1:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Kyv9sHrxV_U&t=113)

- https://github.com/ancsemi/Haven

Haven is a self-hosted Discord alternative that runs on your own machine with no cloud, no email signup, and no tracking. You get text channels, peer-to-peer voice, screen sharing, roles and reactions, and people join with an invite code. Direct messages can be endto-end encrypted. It comes with a moderation API, scheduled backups, and two-factor login.
