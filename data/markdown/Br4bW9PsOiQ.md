LLM Council, a project that basically turns your favorite AI models into a panel of experts. Instead of asking just one LLM for an answer, this tool sends your question to multiple models at once through Open Router, lets them respond individually, then has them critique and rank each other, all before a designated chairman LLM pieces everything together into one final refined answer. It's like hosting a mini AI debate inside your browser.
