## Keeper

[0:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_j_GisoWXVk&t=9)

- https://github.com/ridafkih/keeper.sh

Managing three separate calendars across Google, Outlook, and iCloud means double bookings are basically inevitable. Keeper is a self-hosted sync tool that bridges all three, pulling events together so your availability is accurate everywhere. It also ships as an MCP server, so your local AI agents can read and manage your schedule directly.

## Grimmory

[0:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_j_GisoWXVk&t=31)

- https://github.com/grimmory-tools/grimmory

Grimmeray is a self-hosted library manager for EPUBS, PDFs, and comics, including CBZ and CBR formats. Built-in browser reader, automatic metadata fetching, and reading progress sync directly to Coobo e-readers and OPDS compatible apps. Your entire digital library in one place with no Amazon account required to access it. For anyone who's been meaning to get their books off Kindle and onto their own hardware, this is the clean solution.

## MiniStack

[0:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_j_GisoWXVk&t=59)

- https://github.com/Nahuel990/ministack

Local stack pulling core services behind a payw wall hit a lot of local development setups hard. Minstack is the free replacement. It emulates 20 AWS services in a single Docker container. But instead of mocking API responses, it spins up real Postgress databases, Reddus instances, and runs SQL through duct DB. 30 megabytes of RAM boots in under two seconds. Point your existing code at port 4566 and nothing else changes.
