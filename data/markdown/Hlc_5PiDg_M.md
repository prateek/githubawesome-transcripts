Try Omari packages the Omari Linux desktop as a native Mac app for Apple silicon running through Apple's hypervisor framework with Metal doing the rendering. It shares your Mac's camera, clipboard, and network ports with the guest OS. This fork's pitch is idle cost. Total idle CPU dropped from 71% of a core to 15, mostly by moving interrupt handling into Apple's hypervisor.
