## nub

[1:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fZ5TgW3u40&t=61)

- https://github.com/nubjs/nub

The day-to-day Node.js tool chain forces you to manage a bloated pile of utilities like Nodeman or NVM. ## PixelRAG

[1:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fZ5TgW3u40&t=86)

- https://github.com/StarTrail-org/PixelRAG

Web parsing breaks the moment a site restructures its HTML. Pixel Rag sidesteps the problem entirely by never parsing in the first place. ## loop-library
