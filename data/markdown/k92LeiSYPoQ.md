Scroll World builds one of those Apple style landing pages where the camera flies through a whole world as you scroll. No cuts, every scene flowing into the next. This skill generates the diarama scenes and camera flights with Higsfield renders each connector using the actual neighboring frames as its end points. So both sides of every seam are pixel identical and the flight never breaks.
