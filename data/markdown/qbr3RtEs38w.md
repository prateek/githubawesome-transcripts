Imagine real time local speech to text in your browser. No cloud, no lag, no nonsense. This is Whisper Live Kit, the open- source toolkit that brings Whisper to life. It gives you live transcription. While normal Whisper models lose context and chop words mid-sentence, this one uses intelligent buffering and incremental processing to keep every word smooth and accurate. It's got a full backend, simple front end, supports multiple users, and even pauses when you're not speaking, saving compute like a boss. Perfect for meetings, podcasts, accessibility, or support calls.
