## Bytebot

[0:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9yb4o2IwkqM&t=26)

- https://github.com/bytebot-ai/bytebot

First up, meet Bitebot, the open-source AI desktop agent that literally uses a computer like a human. This self-hosted runs inside a full Linux desktop environment, automating your tasks through simple natural language commands. You can tell it, download all vendor invoices, and organize them into folders. And Bitebot will open browsers, log into portals, click buttons, type passwords, even handle 2FA, all on its own virtual machine. Unlike browseronly bots or RPA scripts, Bitebot has its own computer complete with apps, files, and a full screen view scaling from one to hundreds of virtual desktops in parallel.

## airi

[1:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9yb4o2IwkqM&t=67)

- https://github.com/moeru-ai/airi

Next up, meet Project ARY, an open- source recreation of Neurosama, the legendary AI VTuber. AI isn't just another chatbot. It's a container of souls, a framework to bring AI companions and digital characters into our world. Think of it as a mix between character, silly tavern, and a VRM powered vtuber all rolled into one. Capable of chatting, playing games, reacting to your screen, and even understanding your code. Built with web technologies like web GPU, web assembly, and web audio, airy runs seamlessly across browsers, desktops, and even mobile devices while supporting CUDA and Metal for serious AI performance.

## Rybbit

[1:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9yb4o2IwkqM&t=109)

- https://github.com/rybbit-io/rybbit

If you've looking for a privacyfriendly alternative to Google Analytics, meet Ribbit. The modern open-source analytics platform. Ribbit gives you all the essentials: sessions, users, bounce rate, funnels, and retention tracking, but without cookies or invasive tracking. You can host it yourself for full control, or jump into their freehosted tier to get started in minutes. Plus, Ribbit goes beyond traditional analytics with real-time dashboards, custom event tracking, and even session replays, so you can actually see how users interact with your app without compromising their privacy.

## HeadlessX

[3:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9yb4o2IwkqM&t=189)

- https://github.com/saifyxpro/HeadlessX

Meet Headless X, a browserless automation server built for web automation and data extraction. It's made for developers who need a fast API first scraping engine, subsecond responses, playright under the hood, and REST endpoints that plug into Zapier, NADN, or any HTTP client. Its focus on realistic browsing behavior and consistency across device profiles. Everything from natural mouse and scroll patterns to detailed device fingerprints designed to make automation appear human-like. Headless X packages a lot of power for legitimate use cases. Automated testing, archival scraping of openlyliced content, monitoring your own web properties, or integrating screenshots and PDF generation into pipelines.

## HomeHub

[3:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9yb4o2IwkqM&t=237)

- https://github.com/surajverma/homehub

Next up, meet HomeHub, your all-in-one family dashboard for the self-hosted home. Imagine having one private space on your home network where your family can share notes, track chores, manage shopping lists, and even download media, all without relying on the cloud. Homehub turns any computer, even a Raspberry Pi, into a smart family hub. It's privacy first, simple to install, and designed for everyone in the family, not just the techsavvy ones.

## Glass Keep

[4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9yb4o2IwkqM&t=277)

- https://github.com/nikunjsingh93/react-glass-keep

Next on our list, Glass Keep. A stunning open-source notes app that feels like Google Keep and completely self-hosted. Built with Vite plus React on the front and express plus SQLite on the back. ## Dockpeek

[5:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9yb4o2IwkqM&t=315)

- https://github.com/dockpeek/dockpeek

If you run Docker at home or in the cloud, you're going to love this one. Meet Doc Peak, a self-hosted Docker dashboard that focuses on what matters most, simplicity and speed. It gives you an instant overview of all your containers, ports, and web interfaces. No complex setup, no endless configs. Just deploy it and it works. Doc Peak automatically integrates with Trafix, showing you live container addresses and even supports multi-host management, so you can monitor multiple Docker nodes from one clean dashboard. And yes, it's got one-click updates. Check for new image versions and upgrade your containers right from the UI.

## Termix

- https://github.com/LukeGus/Termix

Ever wished you could manage all your servers, terminals, tunnels, and files from one beautiful web dashboard? Meet Termix, the all-in-one self-hosted server management platform built for developers who live on SSH. Termix gives you full SSH terminal access, a powerful file manager, and even SSH tunneling, all inside your browser. No extra tools needed. View images, videos, code, or markdown directly. even compare files side by side with a simple drag.

## chhoto-url

[10:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9yb4o2IwkqM&t=637)

- https://github.com/SinTan1729/chhoto-url

Last but not least, Chto URL, a tiny lightning fast self-hosted URL shortener built for simplicity and speed. Choto, meaning small in Bangla, lives up to its name. The Docker image is under 6 megabytes. RAM usage stays below 15 megabytes and setup is a breeze. Shorten URLs of any length, track hits in a privacy respecting way, generate QR codes, and even customize or expire links automatically. All without bloated features or unnecessary overhead. Written in Rust with a minimal HTMLJS front end, Choto URL gives you full control. Public mode, API access, hashed passwords, or a private landing page.

All self-hosted, all yours.
