## Attention-Residuals

[1:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v-6nm3gnh6g&t=91)

- https://github.com/MoonshotAI/Attention-Residuals

The team at Moonshot AI just dropped attention residuals. In modern LLMs, standard residual connections accumulate outputs uniformly, which eventually dilutes the information from early layers and limits how deep we can scale models. This paper and code provide a drop-in replacement that uses depthwise softmax attention instead. It fixes the pre-norm dilution crisis and yields a massive 1.25x compute efficiency advantage.
