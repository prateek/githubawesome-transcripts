OCRbase, the self-hosted engine that turns your messy PDFs into usable JSON. It wraps powerful tools like Paddle OCR and local LLMs into a slick API that extracts structured data from documents with accuracy. You just spin up the Docker container and start blasting images at it. It's perfect for processing invoices, scanning receipts, or digitizing archives while keeping your data private and your costs at zero.
