## Trace Anything

[2:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BCmw283mxBY&t=125)

- https://github.com/ByteDance-Seed/TraceAnything

This one's wild. Trace Anything takes any video, image pair, or even an unstructured image set and turns it into a 4D representation. It maps every pixel across frames into a continuous 3D trajectory. Meaning you can literally track how every pixel moves through time and space. Built by Bite Dance, this PyTorch model estimates trajectory fields in a single forward pass and even lets you explore them interactively in 3D. If you've ever wanted to analyze video motion or reconstruct dynamic 3D scenes, this is one of the most advanced open source tools out right now.

## Paleograph

[5:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BCmw283mxBY&t=341)

- https://github.com/DFin/palaeograph

Next up, Paleograph. A browserbased 3D viewer designed for archaeologists who want to explore excavation data sets right in the field. It takes CSV exports of stratographic layers, finds, and spatial coordinates, and renders each artifact as an interactive object in a 3JS scene, all locally, so your data never leaves the workst.
