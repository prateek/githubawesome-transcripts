TUI store is an is an app store for terminal software, but it doesn't replace the package managers already on your machine. It detects your OS and available tool chains, then ranks compatible install methods across Homebrew, Cargo, UV, Docker, and more. Before anything runs, you see the exact command and whether it's verified, scraped, or guessed. That transparency matters when onekey install would otherwise mean blindly trusting a shell command.
