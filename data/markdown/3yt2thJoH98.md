## The loudness war hits vinyl

[6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3yt2thJoH98&t=390)

Vinyl was supposed to be the last refuge from brickwalled mastering, but a new analysis shows the loudness war has conquered records, too, with production teams flattening vinyl dynamics just like digital. The top reply immediately tore into the mythology, pointing out that vinyl has a fraction of a CD's dynamic range. That cozy, warm analog sound is mostly just compression, low-end distortion, and stylus resonance. It turns out limits are the only thing keeping records from completely falling apart.
