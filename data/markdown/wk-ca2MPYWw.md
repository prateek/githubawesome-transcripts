## portless

[0:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wk-ca2MPYWw&t=12)

- https://github.com/vercel-labs/portless

Local development with port numbers sucks. Port conflicts, forgotten ports, wrong apps on cache tabs. Monor repos make it worse. AI agents guess wrong ports. Local host cookies bleed across apps. Browser history is chaotic. Versel Labs built portless to fix this. One line in package.json. Portless myapp next dev. Now your app is at my app localhost 1355. Stable URLs, no conflicts.
