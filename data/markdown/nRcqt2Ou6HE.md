Ever looked at your system and thought, "Why is this even running?" Meet Witter, short for why is this running? It answers one question and it answers it clearly. Instead of dumping raw output like PS or systemctl, whiter explains the cause. What started this process? What's keeping it alive? Pointed at a process name or even a port and Whiter traces everything back to the root in one clean human readable explanation.
