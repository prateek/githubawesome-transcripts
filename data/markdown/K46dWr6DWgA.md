Backpass treats agents.mmd like a model you improve from its own mistakes. It finds local coding agent transcripts, strips tool noise and obvious secrets, then looks for repeated failures that justify a small instruction change. Every proposal includes quotes from real sessions. New rules need evidence from at least two sessions, and nothing writes until you approve
