Booktoskill converts a technical book or documentation folder into an agent skill that loads only the chapter needed for a question. A local extractor cleans the source. Then the generator builds a compact skill file, chapter notes, glossery, patterns, and quick reference rules. It supports PDFs, EPUBs, office documents, and plain text with OCR required for scanned PDFs.
