If you're running agents, you fire every request at one expensive model, even the trivial ones, and burn money. Weave router is a drop-in proxy that picks the right model per request with a tiny Onbox embedter, not vibes. Point clawed code or cursor at it, speak any provider's API, and your keys stay encrypted on your box. The team clocks routing under 50 milliseconds and claims 40 to 70% lower costs.
