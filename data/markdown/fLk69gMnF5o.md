Tiny Claw connects Claude code to WhatsApp for conversational AI responses. Scan a QR code, send messages from your phone, and get clawed responses with persistent context. Uses a file-based Q to process messages sequentially without race conditions. Runs in T-Mux with four panes: WhatsApp client, Q processor, heartbeat monitor, and logs. Heartbeat sends periodic checks to keep tasks
