Need to understand anything fast? Meet Summarize. A fast CLI that gives you the gist of any URL or file. Point it at a web article, a YouTube link, or even PDFs, images, audio, or video, local or remote, and it just works. Blocked website? It falls back automatically. No transcript? It tries again with backups. As it runs, it streams the summary live, beautifully rendered in your terminal with Markdown
