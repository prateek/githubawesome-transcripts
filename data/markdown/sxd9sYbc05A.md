## FleetCode

[0:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sxd9sYbc05A&t=23)

- https://github.com/built-by-as/FleetCode

## Tugtainer

[1:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sxd9sYbc05A&t=89)

- https://github.com/Quenary/tugtainer

If you're a Docker user, you're going to love this one. Introducing Tugtainer, a self-hosted app that automates Docker container updates with a clean web UI on top. Think of it like watchtowwer with a dashboard that lets you see container status, adjust settings, and control updates manually or on a schedule. ## hink

[5:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sxd9sYbc05A&t=358)

- https://github.com/ccbikai/hink

Next in the lineup, meet Hink, a link shortener that's clever and minimal. Instead of a database, Hink uses the hash of a get empty commit as the short ID and stores the original long URL in the commit message. When someone hits the short link, the service fetches the corresponding patch file from GitHub, extracts the URL from the commit message and redirects. Simple, elegant, and heavily cachable. It ships as tiny serverless scripts you can drop into Cloudflare workers. So you get a blazing fast redirect on a single endpoint and built-in analytics via your W.

## WindowMode

[7:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sxd9sYbc05A&t=432)

- https://github.com/True3DLabs/WindowMode

Window mode turns your screen into a window into another world. Using just your webcam, it tracks your face in real time, dynamically rerendering a 3D scene from your perspective. So, it feels like you're peering through a glass portal into a virtual space. No VR headset, no depth sensors, just your face, your screen, and a whole lot of math. The magic happens through Media Pipes face landmarker, which estimates your eye position and distance from the camera. Then it recalculates an off-axis projection matrix, giving the illusion of true 3D depth.
