## DeepSeek-OCR

[0:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xlrwDo-nacU&t=22)

- https://github.com/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-OCR

First up, DeepS OCR approach to long context compression through optical mapping. Current LLM struggle with very long text sequences consuming massive GPU memory due to quadratic scaling. Deepsee OCR addresses this by compressing text into vision tokens using the deep encoder to maintain efficiency without sacrificing accuracy. Deepseek OCR shines in benchmarks on omnidok bench. It outperforms God OCR 2.0 and minor U2.0. Beyond OCR, Deepseek OCR can parse charts, chemical formulas, geometric figures, and images.

## LightReasoner

[11:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xlrwDo-nacU&t=660)

- https://github.com/HKUDS/LightReasoner

Instead of large language models teaching small ones, small models can now teach large models, dramatically improving reasoning accuracy while slashing computational costs. By strategically focusing on the critical 10% of reasoning steps, light reasoner boosts performance across multiple benchmarks. Think plus 28% on GSM8K while using 90% less training time, 80% fewer problems, and 99% fewer tune tokens than traditional fine-tuning. Its three-stage framework, critical step selection, contrastive supervision, and self-distillation, lets small models identify the key reasoning moments for larger models to focus on.
