Need to send files fast, privately, and without limits? Alt SendMe is a cross-platform peer-to-peer file transfer app that makes sharing files totally frictionless. No accounts, no cloud middlemen, just direct encrypted transfers from your machine to anyone, anywhere. It's powered by IRO for reliablenat hole punching, quick plus TLS 1.3 for end-to-end security, and supports resumable downloads. Think of it as AirDrop for everyone. Open source, unlimited, and totally private.
