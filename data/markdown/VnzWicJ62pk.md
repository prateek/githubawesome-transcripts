This CRM puts a durable research agent at the center with the database serving as its notebook. It runs on its own deployment and schedule, leasing rows from a work, so closing the browser doesn't stop it. The rule it never breaks is that nothing about a person is guessed. No tool accepts a confidence score since a model grading its own certainty will flatter itself. Weak evidence becomes a suggestion.
