## rustbpe

[2:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rxPzZKCjIRA&t=122)

- https://github.com/karpathy/rustbpe

Tik Token is amazing for inference, but you can't train tokenizers with it. Hugging face can train tokenizers, but it's massive and slow. Rust BP fills that exact gap. It's a lightweight Rust library for training GPT style BPE tokenizers built for speed and simplicity. Training runs in parallel using rayon with GPT4 style regex pre-tokenization out of the box. Once training is done, you export directly to Tik Tok in format.

## NeuroCore

[5:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rxPzZKCjIRA&t=309)

- https://github.com/farukalpay/NeuroCore

Neuroore, a Mac OS utility for structural analysis and visualization of binary files. ## Deep Delta Learning

[6:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rxPzZKCjIRA&t=362)

- https://github.com/yifanzhang-pro/deep-delta-learning

deep delta learning approach to residual networks that goes beyond simple additive shortcuts.
