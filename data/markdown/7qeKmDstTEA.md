Animated handwriting effects on landing pages usually mean hours of manually tracing SVG paths in Illustrator. Tagakei automates the entire process. Give it any Google font and it generates a perfectly animated real-time handwriting effect. The creator wrote a complete image processing pipeline including custom rasterization and distance transforms. Single npm package react components included.
