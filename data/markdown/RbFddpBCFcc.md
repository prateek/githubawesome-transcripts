Brain toQword reconstructs the exact sentence someone typed straight from their brain activity. No implant required. This isn't decoding vague intent. It's recovering the actual words keystroke by keystroke from a participant sitting in an MEG or EEG scanner. The pipeline runs a convolutional encoder into a transformer. Then a character level language model cleans up the output.
