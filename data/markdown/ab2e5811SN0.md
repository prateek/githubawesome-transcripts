## learnharness

[0:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ab2e5811SN0&t=11)

- https://github.com/amosblomqvist/learnharness

Learn Harness is a desktop app where AI agents help you learn something difficult. First, it measures what you already understand. Everything you learn goes into a knowledge graph that carries over between sessions.
