## llmwalk

[4:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BdR4STPI2ns&t=250)

- https://github.com/samwho/llmwalk

LMW walk lets you explore the full answer space of a prompt. It branches across the most likely tokens, then ranks complete answers by probability.
