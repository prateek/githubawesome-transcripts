## Context Engineering

## Dolphin

[1:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6KV5ts9zvsE&t=84)

- https://github.com/bytedance/Dolphin

Document images are one of the hardest things for AI to understand. Text, tables, figures, formulas, layouts. Everything is mixed together. Dolphin is a document parsing model built specifically to solve that. Instead of treating every page like plain OCR, Dolphin follows a smart two-stage process. First, it analyzes the entire page layout and builds a reading order sequence. Then, it parses each element in parallel using specialized anchor prompts. Imagine if your AI tools could talk to each other instead of working alone. That's exactly what PALMCP does.

## Spark-TTS

[3:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6KV5ts9zvsE&t=222)

- https://github.com/SparkAudio/Spark-TTS

Let's talk Spark TTS. One of the most impressive LLM powered texttospech systems out right now. Unlike traditional TTS pipelines that need multiple acoustic models, Spark TTS uses one single LLM to directly predict speech tokens and reconstruct audio, meaning faster inference and way less complexity. What really stands out is zeroot voice cloning. Feed it just a short audio sample and Spark TTS can speak in that voice instantly.
