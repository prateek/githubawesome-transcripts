Need to design stunning isometric diagrams right in your browser? Meet Foss Flow, an open-source progressive web app built with React and the ISOFlow library. It runs completely offline, supports Docker deployment, and now includes full multilingual support across eight languages. You can import your own icons, switch between 3D isometric and flat 2D styles, and even store your diagrams on a server for access across devices. Whether you're sketching system architecture or network topologies, Foss Flow turns your ideas into clean, professional looking 3D diagrams, all in your browser.
