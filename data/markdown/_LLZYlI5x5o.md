## ViPE

[2:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_LLZYlI5x5o&t=134)

- https://github.com/nv-tlabs/vipe

Next is VIPE, the video pose engine from Nvidia. Designed for geometric 3D perception, VIP takes raw videos, dash cam cinematic shots, even 360° panoramas, and annotates them with camera poses and dense depth maps. By combining dense optical flow, slam based point tracking, and moninocular depth networks, VIP produces scale consistent 3D reconstructions. The project also comes with a massive annotated data set of 96 million frames, accelerating spatial AI research for robotics, ARVR, and 3D vision systems. \[Music\]

## Flash Sparse Attention

[2:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_LLZYlI5x5o&t=171)

- https://github.com/Relaxed-System-Lab/Flash-Sparse-Attention

Halfway through our list is Flash Sparse Attention, a nextg kernel that makes sparse attention blazing fast on modern GPUs. Traditional sparse attention wastes compute and memory, especially on small group sizes. FSA flips the kernel loops and splits computation into three specialized kernels, drastically reducing memory access and boosting speed. It's optimized for NVIDIA ampear and Hopper GPUs, supports FP16, BF-16, and works for both training and inference on large language models.

## SpikingBrain

[5:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_LLZYlI5x5o&t=306)

- https://github.com/BICLab/SpikingBrain-7B

Number nine is spiking brain. A brain inspired approach to large AI models. It combines hybrid efficient attention, mixture of experts modules, and spike encoding to achieve top tier performance with less than 2% of typical training data optimized for non-NVIDIA clusters. Spiking Brain supports large-scale training and inference while achieving over 100 times speed up in TTFT for form token sequences. The repo includes the full spiking brain 7B implementation and weights, including hugging face, VLM inference, and quantized versions. Neuromorphic inspired AI meets efficiency. This one's a must check.
