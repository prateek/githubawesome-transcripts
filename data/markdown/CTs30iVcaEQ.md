## ghostty-blackhole

[0:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CTs30iVcaEQ&t=11)

- https://github.com/s0xDk/ghostty-blackhole

Wrangling complex clawed code sessions usually means constantly checking text percentages to see when your context window is about to overflow. Ghosty black hole turns your context limits into a literal rayraced black hole inside your terminal. It uses a custom ghosty shader to gravitationally lens and distort your terminal text live as your token count fills up. Running compact shrinks the hole back to a tiny seed in the corner.

[0:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CTs30iVcaEQ&t=39)

## loop-engineering

[1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CTs30iVcaEQ&t=63)

- https://github.com/cobusgreyling/loop-engineering

Prompt engineering was about getting one good answer. Loop engineering is the next layer. Designing the whole system that prompts and orchestrates an AI coding agent through many cycles of plan, act, check, and correct. This repo is a practical reference for exactly that. The patterns for building durable agent loops. Drawing on work from Adi Osmani and Anthropics Boris Churnney.

## Bernini
