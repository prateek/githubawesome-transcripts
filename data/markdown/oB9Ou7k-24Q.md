## HN Time Capsule

[0:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oB9Ou7k-24Q&t=37)

- https://github.com/karpathy/hn-time-capsule

HN Time Capsule automatically fetches the hacker news front page from the same day a decade back. It grabs the original articles, every comment, and then prompts an LLM to evaluate those discussions with hindsight. It even grades commenters on how accurate or precient their predictions were. Who called it right, who got it hilariously wrong, and which discussions aged like fine wine or spoiled milk. Finally, it generates a full HTML report summarizing everything.

## Device Activity Tracker

[1:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oB9Ou7k-24Q&t=95)

- https://github.com/gommzystudio/device-activity-tracker

Your WhatsApp activity is leaking more than you think. This tool, device activity tracker, shows how delivery receipts plus RTT timing can reveal exactly when someone's phone is active, idle, or offline without ever touching their device. Based on the careless whisper research paper, it measures roundtrip times of message delivery receipts to detect when a device is being used in real time, when it's in standby, possible location or network changes, and long-term activity patterns.

## stoolap

[3:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oB9Ou7k-24Q&t=207)

- https://github.com/stoolap/stoolap

Stoolap, a modern embedded SQL database written in Rust.
