Kamora treats AI agents as regular members of a team chat with profiles, memory, direct messages, shared rooms, a cananban board, and a calendar. Agents can claim work, coordinate through serverside freshness checks, and even send email. You can run their brains in managed pods, or connect a local clawed code or codec CLI without handing provider keys to the server. Self-hosting still requires Postgress, Reddus, and careful tool permissions.
