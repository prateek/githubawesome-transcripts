Tonell is a LLM friendly serialization format that can cut your data size by up to 50 to 60% compared to JSON, which also means dramatically lower LLM token costs. But Tonell isn't just a format. It's a full-blown data plat. You get a JSON path style query API, CRUD modification tools, hashing and B tree indexes, and even bit packing for boolean data. It's zero dependencies, built-in pure TypeScript, browser ready at just 10 kbgzipped.
