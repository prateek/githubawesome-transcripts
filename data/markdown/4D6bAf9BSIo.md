## whatcable

[0:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4D6bAf9BSIo&t=35)

- https://github.com/darrylmorley/whatcable

USBC cables are a total mess. One charges at 5 watts, another does 100 watts with Thunderbolt 4, and they look identical in your drawer. ## serve-sim

## GooseRelayVPN

[3:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4D6bAf9BSIo&t=209)

- https://github.com/Kianmhz/GooseRelayVPN

A wildly clever networking hack. Goose RelayVPN is a SOCOX 5VPN that literally tunnels your raw TCP traffic through Google Appcript to reach your VPS exit server.
