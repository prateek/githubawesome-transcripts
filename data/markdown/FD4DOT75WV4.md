Praxist is for the moment a project runs, hits a number, and nobody knows what to try next. You hand it the runnable code and one metric, and it sends several agents down competing paths at once. Each generation feeding what worked into the next instead of starting cold. Failed approaches come back as evidence you can act on, not silence.
