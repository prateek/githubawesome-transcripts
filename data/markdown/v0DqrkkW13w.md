## e2ecp

## linux-wasm

- https://github.com/joelseverin/linux-wasm

What if you could boot Linux in your browser? No VM, no native install, just pure web assembly. That's Linux WASM, a wild experiment that compiles a full Linux stack to WASM. ## Kimi Linear

[3:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v0DqrkkW13w&t=231)

- https://github.com/MoonshotAI/Kimi-Linear

Ever wanted linear attention that doesn't compromise performance, even on million token contexts? Kim Linear is that architecture. At its core is Kimmy Delta attention, a refined take on gated Deltaet with smarter gating to squeeze the most out of finite state RNN memory. It's a hybrid attention system, 3:1 KDA to global MLA, which slashes memory usage without losing quality. Think up to 75% smaller KV caches and six ties faster decoding, all while handling long cover short and RL style tasks with ease.

## Glyph

[4:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v0DqrkkW13w&t=264)

- https://github.com/thu-coai/Glyph

Ever hit the ceiling on long context models and thought there's got to be a smarter way? Glyph flips the script. Instead of feeding millions of tokens directly into a model, it renders text as images and processes them with vision language models. This visual text compression turns the long context problem into a multimodal task, slashing memory usage and compute costs without losing the meaning of your text. Glyph achieves massive token compression, speeds up inference, and still hits competitive benchmarks on LongBench and MRCR, even with 128K token inputs.
