Human Atlas lets you take apart a 3D human body in your browser. You can toggle anatomical systems, search for structures, and spread visible pieces into an exploded view to see how they fit together. Built with React and 3JS, it runs locally without accounts or API keys and deploys as a static site. Its anatomy comes from an adult male reference.
