## bigset

## MisoTTS

[0:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wBMLiN-fclo&t=36)

Most text to speech still sounds flat, that dead robotic narration that gives away an AI voiceover instantly. ## image-extender

## nbd-vram
