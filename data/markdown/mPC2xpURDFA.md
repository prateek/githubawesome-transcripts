Visos is a portfolio carousel where the whole ring is one full screen fragment shader, not a grid of DOM elements. Card positions arrive as uniforms and the shader blends their signed distance fields with a smooth minimum. So neighbors melt together as they close and string thinning threads as they part. Clicking a card centers it, but opens nothing yet.
