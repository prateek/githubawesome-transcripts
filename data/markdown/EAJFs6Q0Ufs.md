Shad and Lint turns your Tailwind design rules into checks. Coding agents can run. You define which styles a component can change. Then violations explain what went wrong and suggest alternatives from your existing design system. A button with forbidden padding can point the agent toward its size variance instead. It works with ESLint or Oxlint on Tailwind V4 projects, even if you don't use Shad UI.
